## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] Written authorization is on file and covers the exact hosts in scope.
- [ ] The target is a dedicated test environment, not production.
- [ ] Test accounts and roles are documented.
- [ ] Backups and rollback steps are confirmed with the owner.
- [ ] Rate limits and maintenance windows are agreed.
- [ ] A communication channel and emergency contact are available.
- [ ] Evidence handling rules are agreed (redaction, storage, retention).
- [ ] The scan will stop immediately if instability or data exposure is observed.
